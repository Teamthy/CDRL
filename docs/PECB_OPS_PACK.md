## 1. Press Release — Partnership Announcement

**Send to:** PECB Partner Manager / press@pecb.com if your partnership pitch requires
PECB-hosted publication; also distribute to your own channels after PECB approval.

**Subject:** Press Release — Ykay Consulting Hub joins the PECB network as an Authorized Partner

```text
LAGOS, Nigeria — [Date]

Ykay Consulting Hub (Centre for Digital Risk & Leadership) today announced its
partnership with PECB, the global certification body for management systems and
professional competencies, becoming a PECB Authorized Partner.

Through this partnership, Ykay Consulting Hub will deliver PECB Certified training
courses across information security, cybersecurity, privacy & data protection,
business continuity, governance, risk & compliance, quality, health & safety,
sustainability, and artificial intelligence governance — including ISO/IEC 27001,
ISO 22301, ISO 9001, ISO 45001, GDPR, and ISO/IEC 42001 programmes — concluding
with official PECB certification exams.

"Digital risk is now an enterprise leadership mandate across Africa," said
[Name], [Title], Ykay Consulting Hub. "This partnership brings globally
recognized, exam-certified pathways to professionals and organizations who
need credible proof of competence — delivered locally, recognized worldwide."

PECB certified credentials are issued directly by PECB and are verifiable on
the PECB website. Course schedules, delivery formats (virtual, in-person,
hybrid), and enrollment are available at https://ykayconsultinghub.com.ng/training.

About Ykay Consulting Hub
Centre for Digital Risk & Leadership (Ykay Consulting Hub) is a Lagos-based
professional education, certification, and advisory institution focused on
cybersecurity, digital governance, AI risk, and leadership development for
African organizations.

About PECB
PECB is a certification body providing education and certification under
ISO/IEC 17024 for individuals on a wide range of disciplines, including
management systems, cybersecurity, privacy, and artificial intelligence.
Learn more at https://pecb.com.

Media contact
[Name] · Ykay Consulting Hub · info@ykayconsultinghub.com.ng · [Phone]
```

**Approval note:** email this draft to your PECB Partner Manager *before* public
distribution — PECB asks partners to have partnership-related announcements
reviewed to ensure naming/brand compliance.

## 2. Partner-Side Exam Request (Course Completion → Exam Provisioning)

Used after a Ykay-run course ends, to request PECB exam access for candidates.

**Send to:** PECB Partner Manager / exams@pecb.com

**Subject:** Exam request — [Course Code] — [Delivery dates] — [n] candidates

```text
Dear PECB Exams Team,

Following completion of our [Course Code and full title] training course,
delivered [dates] via [Virtual/Live Virtual/In-person] by [Trainer name],
we kindly request exam provisioning for the candidates listed below
(attached spreadsheet; fields per your standard format).

Partner:      Ykay Consulting Hub — Centre for Digital Risk & Leadership
Partner ID:   [your PECB Partner Account ID]
PO/credit ref: [PO number or "exam credits — please advise balance"]

Candidate list per row: First name | Last name | Email (must match candidate's
PECB profile) | [Course code] | Preferred exam language.

Requested exam window: [date range].
Exam format: [Online proctored / Paper-based at partner site / at exam center].

Please confirm receipt and the expected provisioning date so we can brief
candidates on exam rules, scheduling, and system requirements.

Kind regards,
[Name] · Partnerships Lead · Ykay Consulting Hub
info@ykayconsultinghub.com.ng · [Phone]
```

### Internal checklist before sending
1. Every candidate's email matches their PECB web account (mismatches block provisioning).
2. Confirm course attendance records are complete (PECB requires full training completion).
3. Confirm credit balance / PO covers `candidates × exam units` (per your partnership level scheme).
4. Share with candidates: exam rules, ID requirements, system check link, and the
   retake policy (one free retake per PECB policy — verify current terms with your PM).

## Brand-use quick reference (keep near any template)
- ✅ Say: "PECB Authorized Partner", "PECB Certified training courses", "delivered
  by Ykay Consulting Hub", "certification exam issued by PECB".
- ❌ Never imply: PECB ownership of Ykay, exclusive rights, or that PECB endorses
  your other (non-PECB) services.
- Logo: use only the partner logo files from your PECB partner account resources,
  unmodified, hyperlinking to pecb.com where feasible.
